03 / PHILOSOPHY & BACKGROUND

WHO WE ARE

We are Flora and Nico, known worldwide as Duo Rings: a couple of high-performance athletes, artists and entrepreneurs. Creators of Duo Rings, a one-of-a-kind aerial rings act, and of The Rings Method, the first functional training method with gymnastics rings for all levels.

In high performance, the key is not training more, but understanding the body and training functionally and consciously, so your body can support you for life — preventing injuries and thinking about longevity.

“Rings are our life. We risk everything in every show, and that's why we know how to prepare the mind and body for high performance.”

We want to share what we know to help more people improve their quality of life through movement with rings.

WELLNESS IS THE NEW LUXURY.
