Alto Rendimiento Sin Burnout

El propósito principal es guiar a las personas hacia el high level performance educativo, profesional y empresarial, ayudándolas a comprender el wellness y la importancia fundamental de balancear la calidad de vida con el trabajo, para resolver problemas de manera creativa.

Un espacio clave para aprender a combatir la ansiedad, el estrés, el burnout y el dolor corporal derivado del sedentarismo, el stress y la ansiedad.

Metodología & Enfoque

El Enfoque de la Masterclass

La propuesta integra conceptos de liderazgo, coaching, alto rendimiento, presencia escénica y habilidades blandas (soft skills), conectando herramientas académicas con la experiencia real.

Una propuesta construida para generar una conexión académica y humana con la audiencia, destacando la importancia fundamental del respeto, la gratitud y la amabilidad como pilares indispensables para crear un ambiente laboral o de estudio óptimo. Demostramos que el verdadero alto rendimiento no depende solo de cuánto podemos hacer, sino de cómo pensamos, cómo cuidamos nuestros cuerpos, cómo gestionamos nuestras emociones y cómo nos relacionamos con los demás desde la empatía.

Biografías Oficiales

Quiénes Somos

Co-founder & Speaker

Flora Aracama

A former member of Argentina's National Team for over 10 years, Flora began training at age 4 and built an international career that includes national and international medals, among them a silver medal at the ODESUR Games.

She was the female lead of SEP7IMO DÍA – No Descansaré, the Cirque du Soleil show inspired by Soda Stereo, where she played La Femme Solaire.

She studied Business Administration at UBA and holds a Master's in Arts & Culture Management from Rome Business School. She also trained in Mindfulness in Rome and earned Harvard's certification in The Power of Influence for Positive Impact.

Passionate about strategy, marketing, communication and experience design, she is co-founder of Duo Rings and The Rings Method. Her career blends high performance, artistic sensibility, strategy and a strong stage presence.

Co-founder & Speaker

Nico Busso

A former member of Argentina's National Team for over 10 years, Nico began training at age 6 and reached national and international titles. His specialty was the rings — a discipline that would shape much of his professional career.

After starting his studies in Engineering, he discovered his true passion: understanding the body and helping prevent and treat the injuries and pain associated with high performance. He graduated in Kinesiology and Physiatry from UAI and continued specializing internationally in mobility, prevention and sports performance.

He specializes in manual therapies and techniques such as MEP, POLD and Neurofascial Taping, and works with high-performance athletes from around the world.

He was captain of the Cirque du Soleil team and is co-founder of Duo Rings and The Rings Method.

Detail-oriented, empathetic and deeply connected to movement, he combines his experience as an athlete with a holistic view of the body and performance.
